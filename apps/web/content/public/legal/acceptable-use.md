Use public Fuma surfaces lawfully and without harming people, systems, or rights. Product-specific acceptable-use terms may add rules before a service is activated.

## Prohibited activity

Do not use Fuma surfaces to distribute malware; phish; spam; impersonate; harass; exploit or endanger children; violate privacy; infringe rights; evade access, rate, or payment controls; manipulate traffic; or disrupt a service.

Do not test with another person’s account or data, run destructive or high-volume security activity, upload live secrets or harmful payloads, or keep information reached without authorization.

## Public forms

Use Security reporting for a vulnerability concern and Contact for a concise abuse report. Include only the public URL or identifier and the minimum context needed. Form acceptance does not confirm delivery, investigation, outcome, or response time.

## Safety response

An owning authority may reject or limit a request that violates these rules or threatens safety. Any product restriction, suspension, appeal, evidence retention, or legal action requires the applicable authority and product terms; this public website does not claim those operations have occurred.

## Review state

Independent legal and trust-and-safety approval for production launch remains pending. This notice makes no certification, monitoring-coverage, enforcement-speed, or regulatory claim.
