Use Fuma services lawfully and with respect for other people, systems and rights.

## Prohibited activity

Do not distribute malware, phish, impersonate, harass, exploit children, violate privacy, infringe rights, evade service limits or disrupt the platform.

## Reporting

Use the security route for vulnerability reports and the contact route for abuse reports. Include only the information needed to investigate safely.

## Enforcement

Fuma may limit or suspend abusive activity while reviewing context and applicable obligations.
