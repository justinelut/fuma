The public website does not use an application, administration, member or tenant session cookie.

## Essential operation

Security, routing and availability controls may process short-lived request information without optional storage.

## Optional measurement

If you allow optional measurement, the public site records a versioned choice in session storage for this host. It is not a parent-domain cookie and is not available to product or tenant hosts.

## Change your choice

Clear the site session or use the privacy control to withdraw optional measurement. Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track always disables the optional path.
