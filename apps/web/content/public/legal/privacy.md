This notice covers the Fuma public website. It does not describe a signed customer contract, a production provider arrangement, or every product, member, tenant, payment, or administration workflow.

## Information you choose to send

A contact form asks for your name, reply email, message, request type, and consent to the current privacy notice. Security, privacy, abuse, and expert enquiry forms may also ask for a relevant category or listing. Do not send passwords, secret keys, identity documents, payment details, health information, or other sensitive records.

The form checks required fields, message size, and unsafe input before accepting your request. An accepted response confirms that the form passed those checks; it does not promise a response time.

## Anti-abuse processing

The public website uses limited anti-abuse checks such as a hidden field, submission timing, replay protection, and short rate limits. These checks use privacy-minimized identifiers and do not keep the contact message in the replay store.

## Measurement and browser storage

Browsing the public website does not require an account cookie. Optional measurement remains off until you choose it. Your preference is stored for this browsing session. Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track keeps optional measurement off.

## Retention and production approval

The short-lived anti-abuse records described above are kept only for their stated protection period. Contact retention, deletion schedules, legal holds, delivery providers, and production ownership still require review before launch.

## Requests and changes

Use the Privacy request page to ask about access, correction, deletion, or this notice. A separate identity-verification process may be needed. Submission does not confirm eligibility, completion, or a deadline. The policy history page records the current version and will identify superseded public versions when any exist.
