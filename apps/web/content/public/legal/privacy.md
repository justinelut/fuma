This public website minimises collection. Essential operational requests do not require a cross-site identifier, and optional measurement remains off unless you choose it.

## Information you submit

When you contact Fuma, we process the name, reply address and message you provide for the purpose shown on the form. Do not include secrets, credentials or sensitive personal information.

## Measurement choices

We respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Optional measurement uses a host-only session preference and stops when you withdraw consent.

## Retention and requests

Operational and contact records are retained only for their documented purpose and bounded retention period. Use the privacy contact route to request access or deletion.
