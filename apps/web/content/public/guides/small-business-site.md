Begin with the decision a visitor needs to make. A clear business site usually needs fewer pages than its first outline suggests.

## Name the outcome

Write one sentence describing what a good visitor should understand or do. Use it to test every section.

## Build the minimum path

- Explain the service in plain language
- Show credible examples
- Answer common concerns
- Make contact easy

## Check the result

Review the site at a narrow viewport, zoom the browser, use the keyboard, and read the page without images before publishing.
