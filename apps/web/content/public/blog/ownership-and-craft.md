Ownership is easiest to promise at the end and hardest to preserve throughout a product. We think it should shape the editor, data model and publisher from the beginning.

## Output is part of the interface

A visitor experiences the page, not the builder. Semantic structure, compact styles and predictable loading are therefore part of the authoring experience.

## Portability disciplines the system

When content and design can be understood outside the editor, internal convenience is less likely to leak into the public page. That constraint improves both performance and longevity.
