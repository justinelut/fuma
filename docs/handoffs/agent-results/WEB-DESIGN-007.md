## Result

Recomposed the complete `/pricing` route as an authority-backed commercial record rather than generic pricing-card wallpaper. The existing `PricingCatalog` remains the sole renderer of plan amounts, promotions, allowances, catalog versions, checkout availability, comparison semantics, and unavailable states; the route now gives that contract a clear product narrative and premium Fuma landing-page composition.

## Design rationale

- **Page job:** let a visitor inspect the current published catalog while understanding exactly why a price or purchase action may be absent.
- **Visual signature:** a genuine three-gate pricing sequence—**Published → Current → Re-resolved**. It maps the real commercial path from authority publication, through effective-window filtering, to application-side checkout resolution.
- **Narrative:** authority thesis hero → three-gate provenance rail → framed commercial record → safe-absence ledger → journey-first close.
- **Commerce integrity:** the page does not calculate, duplicate, cache, remember, or invent any price. It delegates all commercial display and purchase actions to `PricingCatalog` and the existing public projection boundary.
- **Unavailable states:** stale, withdrawn, missing, or commercially incomplete authority remains represented by the catalog’s existing `AuthorityUnavailable` path. The surrounding copy explicitly treats missing values and actions as a safety contract, not a broken layout.
- **Restraint:** no fabricated product UI, generic tier badges, “most popular” treatment, invented discounts, customer proof, unsupported amounts, or decorative state color was introduced. The commercial-record frame and provenance rail are the sole expressive devices.
- **Fuma language:** section rhythm, asymmetric headline/lede grids, measured display typography, semantic surface roles, ledger rows, and concise CTA pairs match the approved homepage, Website, Publication, and Solutions compositions.
- **Positioning:** copy describes one closed, Fuma-owned platform. It contains no self-hosting, open-source, infrastructure-ownership, database, deployment, or licence claims.

## Contracts preserved

- canonical pricing metadata remains generated by `publicMetadata(..., '/pricing')`
- route remains `force-dynamic`
- cadence input remains bounded to `monthly | annual`, defaulting safely to `monthly`
- profile input remains bounded to `website | publication | undefined`
- authority read remains `readPublicData('pricing', { cadence, profile, limit: 100 })`
- `PricingCatalog` still receives the authority envelope, cadence, and profile unchanged
- KES/en-KE formatting remains inside the established authority and formatting components; the page adds no hand-formatted amount
- catalog cards, checkout-disabled status, stale/withdrawn suppression, effective version, promotions, quotas, comparison caption, row/column scopes, and keyboard-scrollable table remain untouched
- plan CTA handoffs remain opaque and version-bound through existing catalog behavior
- one page `h1`, ordered section headings, a genuine ordered sequence, descriptive navigation labels from the authority component, and semantic definition-list rows are retained
- all new styling uses existing semantic global roles; no raw colors, local white/black alpha, arbitrary colored shadows, or inline color/style declarations were added
